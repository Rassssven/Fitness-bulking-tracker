Help Center

Find answers to common questions, manage your plans, and learn how to get the best results from your fitness journey.

Getting Started

How do I create a plan?

Go to the calculator page, complete your information, and generate your personalized plan.

Can I customize my meals?

Yes. After generating your plan, open Customize Plan and add your own meals and exercises.

How do I save a plan?

Use the Save Plan button inside the results page or customize page.

Account & Security

How do I reset my password?

Go to Login → Forgot Password and follow the instructions.

Can I edit my profile?

Yes. Open your dashboard and update your account information.
